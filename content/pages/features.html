Features

Everything between "running low" and "back on the shelf"

Reorder Desk covers the whole buying loop for a Shopify store: what to order, from whom, how many, the purchase order itself, and getting the stock back into Shopify.

Get early access

Reorder list

What to order this week, most urgent first

Every variant gets a status: out of stock, order now, order soon, OK, overstock, or no recent sales. The list opens on the items that need ordering, with totals at the top: how many items, what the order would cost, your stock value and the money in dead stock.

  • Filter by status or supplier, search by name, SKU or barcode
  • Edit any quantity right in the list
  • Set the supplier for many items at once, or mark items as not reordered
  • Download the list as CSV

Explainable forecasts

A "why" for every number

Open any item to see its sales per day, what was sold in the last 7, 28 and 90 days, when it runs out, the full order sum, and a 13-week sales chart. Next to it: the supplier, supplier SKU, cost, case pack, minimum order and lead time for that item.

  • Override sales per day, days to cover, safety stock or the quantity
  • A clear warning when there is too little history to trust
  • Shows which bundles an item is also sold in

Read exactly how the forecast is worked out

Purchase orders

Purchase orders without the copy and paste

Select the items that need ordering and click once: Reorder Desk drafts one purchase order per supplier, or adds to the open draft you already have. Edit lines, add items by search, set the expected date, shipping cost and notes.

  • Email it to the supplier with the PDF and CSV attached, or mark it as sent
  • Your company name, reply-to address, order number prefix and terms on every order
  • Print, download PDF or CSV at any time
  • Back to draft, cancel, close short or reopen

Receiving

Receive in one click, undo in one click

Enter what arrived and Reorder Desk adds it to the stock in Shopify, at the location you receive at. Each delivery is saved as a receipt, so you always know what came in when, and a mistake is fixed with Undo.

  • Partial deliveries: receive what came, the rest stays on order
  • Optionally set Shopify's "cost per item" to the order's cost
  • Safe to retry: the same receipt is never added twice

Suppliers

Suppliers set up from the sheet you already have

Import supplier, supplier SKU, cost, case pack, minimum order, lead time and email from Excel, CSV or a Google Sheet. Columns are recognised by name, including Stocky and Shopify export headers. Preview every change before anything is saved, and download a report of what happened.

  • Match items by SKU or barcode
  • Empty cells never wipe what is already saved
  • No sheet? Create suppliers from your product vendors in one click

And also

The details that make the numbers right

Bundles

Bundles made with Shopify's bundle components are read automatically; for other bundle apps, set the parts once. Every bundle sale counts as sales of its parts, and the bundle itself is never reordered.

Case packs and minimums

Quantities are rounded up to whole case packs, then to the minimum order quantity. Purchase orders warn when the total is under the supplier's minimum order value.

Lead times

Set a lead time per item, per supplier, or one default for the store. An item without its own lead time uses its supplier's, then the store default.

Weekly email and alerts

A weekly "what to order" email on the day and hour you choose, sent only when something needs ordering. A low-stock alert once per item when it newly needs ordering. One-click unsubscribe in every email.

Dead stock

Items with no sale in 90 days (or the number of days you choose), their stock value at cost, and their last sale. Mark the ones you no longer sell as "don't reorder".

Stock on order and in transit

Sent purchase orders count as stock on order, so nothing is ordered twice. Choose whether drafts and Shopify transfers in transit count too.

Locations

Pick which Shopify locations count towards stock and where deliveries are received. Free counts one location; Pro counts as many as you like.

Seasonal forecasts (Pro)

With a year of sales history, the forecast follows last year's pattern for the weeks ahead, such as a festival peak or a quiet summer. The "why" shows the change.

Privacy by design

No customer names, emails or addresses are read. Sales are kept as daily totals per item. Uninstalling deletes your store's data.

What Reorder Desk doesn't do (yet)

We'd rather you know before you install. Reorder Desk is made for stores that buy finished products from suppliers.

  • Manufacturing: raw materials and bills of materials
  • Planning transfers between your own warehouses
  • Syncing purchase orders to accounting software
  • Sales on Amazon and other marketplaces outside Shopify
