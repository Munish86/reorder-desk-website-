How to move over

What changed

Stocky was Shopify's own inventory app, included with Shopify POS Pro. Shopify removed it from the App Store on 2 February 2026 and it stopped working on 31 August 2026. Shopify now points merchants to the inventory tools built into Shopify admin.

Those tools are good at tracking stock, transfers and counts. What they don't do is the part most Stocky users relied on every week: work out what to reorder, and how many, from your sales.

Moving over

From Stocky to your first purchase order

Shopify's help centre notes that Stocky can't export suppliers. So the quickest route is the sheet you already have: an old Stocky report, a Shopify product export, or your suppliers' price lists.

  1. Install Reorder Desk. The first sync reads your products, stock and sales history by itself.
  2. Add suppliers. Click "Create suppliers from vendors" if your product vendors are your suppliers. Otherwise import a sheet with one row per SKU: supplier, and any of supplier SKU, cost, case pack, minimum order and lead time. Columns are recognised by name, including Stocky's and Shopify's headers.
  3. Preview, then import. You see every change before it is saved: which items link to which supplier, and which SKUs weren't found. Empty cells never overwrite what's saved.
  4. Check the defaults. Lead time, days to cover and safety stock in Settings. Give suppliers their own lead times where they differ.
  5. Order. Select everything that needs ordering and click "Create purchase orders". Check each draft, then send it.

Open purchase orders from Stocky can't be copied over automatically. For any still on the way, create the order in Reorder Desk and mark it as sent, so its stock counts as on order and isn't bought twice.

Stocky questions

Can I import my Stocky data directly?

Not automatically: Stocky can't export suppliers, and it no longer runs. Reorder Desk reads your sales history straight from Shopify, so forecasts don't need anything from Stocky. For suppliers, use any sheet that has SKUs and supplier names.

Do I need Shopify POS Pro?

No. Reorder Desk works on any Shopify plan, with or without POS.

Will my forecasts start from zero?

No. Your sales history is in Shopify, not in Stocky, and Reorder Desk reads it on the first sync.
