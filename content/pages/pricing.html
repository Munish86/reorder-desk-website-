Billing questions

How am I billed?

Through Shopify. The charge appears on your Shopify invoice with your other apps, in the currency Shopify bills you in. We never see your card details.

How does the free trial work?

Pro starts with a 14-day free trial. If you cancel or move to Free before it ends, you pay nothing.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, any time from the Plans page in the app. Shopify works out any difference for the current billing period.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Suppliers, purchase orders and settings stay; forecasts are shown for your 50 best sellers and stock is counted at one location again.

And if I uninstall?

Billing stops at once, and your store's data is deleted from Reorder Desk when Shopify asks us to, as described in the privacy policy. Stock you received stays in Shopify.
