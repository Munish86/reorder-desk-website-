The problem

Guessing what to reorder costs you twice

Order too little and your best sellers sell out. Order too much and your cash sits on a shelf. A spreadsheet can't keep up with both.

Stock-outs you see too late

By the time an item shows zero, the supplier still needs weeks to deliver. Those are sales you already paid to win.

Cash stuck in slow stock

Rounding up "just in case" on every order adds up. Items that stopped selling keep being reordered out of habit.

No clear answer after Stocky

Stocky closed on 31 August 2026. Shopify's built-in inventory tools track stock, but don't tell you what to reorder or how much.

How it works

From sales history to a purchase order in three steps

Connect your store

Install from the Shopify App Store. Reorder Desk reads your products, stock per location and sales history, then keeps stock up to date as it changes.

See what to order

The reorder list ranks items by urgency: out of stock, order now, order soon. Each one shows sales per day, when it runs out and how many to buy.

Order and receive

Create one purchase order per supplier, email it as a PDF, and add the stock to Shopify when it arrives, in part or in full.

Explainable forecasts

The reason behind every number

Click "Why?" on any item and see exactly how its order quantity was worked out, in plain sentences. If you know something the data doesn't, change it.

  • Recent weeks count more than older ones
  • Days an item was out of stock are left out, so a stock-out doesn't lower its forecast
  • Lead time, case packs and minimum orders built in
  • Set your own sales rate, cover days or quantity per item

Purchase orders

One order per supplier, ready to send

Tick the items that need ordering and Reorder Desk drafts a purchase order for each supplier, with their SKUs and costs. Send it from the app as a PDF and CSV.

  • Numbered orders (PO-0001) with your company details and terms
  • Warns when an order is under the supplier's minimum
  • Sent orders count as stock on order, so nothing is bought twice

Receiving

Receive stock straight into Shopify

When a delivery arrives, enter what came in. The stock is added in Shopify at once, at the location you choose. Half a delivery? Receive the rest later.

  • Partial or full receiving, as many times as needed
  • Optionally update "cost per item" in Shopify to the order's cost
  • Typed the wrong number? Undo puts the stock back

Questions

How does Reorder Desk decide how much to order?

It works out how much an item sells per day, with recent weeks counting more, then: (lead time + days to cover) × sales per day + safety stock − stock on hand − stock already on order. The result is rounded up to the supplier's case pack and minimum order. The "Why?" panel shows this sum with your own numbers. More on how forecasts work.

Does it use AI?

No. The forecast is plain arithmetic you can check by hand, which is why every number can be explained. You can always override it.

How much sales history does it read?

Up to two years. Shopify gives apps the last 60 days of orders by default; reading older orders needs Shopify's approval, and Reorder Desk switches to the longer history by itself once it has it. Items with little history are marked as a rough guide.

What data does it read? Does it see my customers?

It reads products, stock per location and, from orders, only the date and which items sold at what price. It does not read customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses, and it stores daily totals per item, not individual orders.

Does it change anything in my Shopify store?

Only when you ask it to: adding stock when you receive a purchase order (or taking it back if you undo), and, if you tick the box, updating the cost per item.
