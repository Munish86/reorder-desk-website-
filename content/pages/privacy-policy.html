This policy covers two things: this website, and the Reorder Desk app for Shopify ("the app"). It explains what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

This website

Forms

When you join early access or send a message, we save what you type in the form: your email address and, if you give them, your name, store address, the tool you use today, your number of product variants and your message, together with the page and the time you sent it. We use it only to answer you, and for early access to email you when the app is live. We don't sell it, share it or add you to a newsletter.

To stop spam, the website counts how many forms come from one internet connection in an hour. It keeps only a scrambled code made from the connection's IP address, for one hour, never the address itself.

Cookies and tracking

The website sets no cookies for visitors and uses no advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this website itself, not from third parties. If we add analytics later, this page will say so first.

How long we keep it

Early-access sign-ups are deleted within 12 months of the app going live. Messages are kept for as long as we need them to help you, and at most two years.

The Reorder Desk app

What the app reads from Shopify

  • Products and variants: titles, SKUs, barcodes, prices, cost per item, vendors and bundle components.
  • Stock per location, and location names.
  • Orders: only the date of each order, whether it was cancelled or a test, and which variants were sold in what quantity and at what price. The app does not request or read customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses.

What the app stores

  • Your store's address, name, email, currency and time zone, your plan, and the access token Shopify gives the app.
  • A copy of your variants and stock levels, refreshed every few hours.
  • Daily sales totals per variant (units and value). Individual orders are not stored. Totals older than about two years are deleted.
  • Suppliers, purchase orders, receipts and the settings you enter.
  • Supplier sheets you upload, deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • A log of emails the app sent for you (recipient, subject, result; not the content), kept for 6 months.

Changes the app makes in Shopify

Only when you ask it to: adding stock when you receive a purchase order (or taking it back when you undo a receipt), and, if you tick the option, updating the cost per item.

Emails

The app sends purchase orders to the supplier addresses you enter, and reorder emails and low-stock alerts to the addresses in your settings. It sends no marketing. Emails are delivered through Resend, used only for sending them.

Where the data is kept

On the app's servers at Railway (database and app) and Cloudflare R2 (uploaded sheets), used only to run the app. The data is not sold or shared with anyone else. Payments are handled by Shopify; the app never sees card details.

Deleting your data

When you uninstall the app, it stops all work for your store at once. Shopify then asks the app to delete your store's data, and the app deletes all of it: products, stock, sales totals, suppliers, purchase orders, settings and files. You can also ask for deletion at any time.

Your rights and contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, from this website or the app. Write to [rd_support_email]; we answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.
